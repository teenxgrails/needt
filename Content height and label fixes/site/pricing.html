Questions about money.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing. It is on your machine — it was never anywhere else. The app reverts to the demo day; your files stay where they are and export as plain text.

Is the demo free, or is it a free tier?

It is free, and it does not expire — but it is one day, not a smaller version of the product. A free tier has to be made worse on purpose, and the part we would have to break is placing the day, which is the whole thing. So the day is whole and there is only one of it.

Is Life really for ever?

It is Pro for as long as Needt exists, with no further payment. Three hundred seats is what the current runway needs; the number is not a marketing device and it does not reset.

Do you take VAT?

Where it applies, added at checkout. Invoices come with a VAT number field for businesses.
