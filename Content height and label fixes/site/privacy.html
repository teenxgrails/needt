Privacy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Short, because there is not much to say: your planning data lives on your machine and we do not want a copy.

What stays on your device

Tasks, projects, documents, habits, brief entries and settings are files on your disk. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on sync, and sync is per-item rather than all-or-nothing.

What we hold if you have an account

An email address, a hashed password or an OAuth identifier, your plan and its billing history. Nothing about what you plan.

What a model sees

Two things, and only when you ask: the text of a task you want broken into a first step, and the week's entries when you ask for a summary. Not your calendar, not your documents, not your history. No input is used for training.

Calendars and connected agents

A connected calendar grants read access to your busy times. An agent connected over MCP receives the capabilities you grant it, and every action it takes appears in the app where you can see and undo it.

Analytics

Aggregate, no identifiers, no session recording, and none of it touches the content of what you plan. It answers questions like which screens are opened, never what is on them.

Your rights

Export is plain text and JSON. Deletion removes the account and everything attached to it; the files on your machine remain yours. Write to privacy@needt.app and a person answers.
