How it works

Like an assistant, built in.

It does the planning. You do the work.

01Step 01

Capture in seconds

Just type it the way you'd say it: call the accountant Friday 10am. Needt reads the day, the time and how long it takes straight out of the sentence.

02Step 02

It finds the time

A list tells you what is outstanding. Needt tells you when it happens: it fits each task into your free hours — and if the day will not hold it all, it says what moves instead of quietly keeping both.

03Step 03

Say yes, or change it

The whole day is drawn before it is saved, and every block says whether Needt may move it or you fixed it in place. When Needt moves something, you watch it happen.
