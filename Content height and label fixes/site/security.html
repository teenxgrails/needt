Data

Your day is not our dataset.

A planner sees the most specific thing about a person: what they are actually doing, hour by hour. So the rule is to hold as little of it as the job needs — your day, and not a byte more.

Your day is in your account

Tasks, projects and habits live in your Needt account and follow you to every device you sign in on. Encrypted in transit and at rest.

Files you keep, we cannot read

Anything you choose to keep on your own computer stays on your own computer. It is not uploaded, not indexed, and not something we could hand over if asked.

Calendars are read, not copied

Connecting a calendar grants read access to the times you are busy. Needt does not need the contents of your meetings to know an hour is gone.

The model sees the minimum

Two things go to a model: the text of a task you asked it to break down, and the week's entries when you ask for a summary. Not your calendar, not your documents, not your history. Nothing is used for training.

The AI only does what you allow

Connect an outside assistant and it gets exactly the permissions you give it. Everything it does shows up as a change you can see and undo. Nothing writes silently.

Leaving takes one click

Export is plain text and JSON, not a format only we can open. Delete the account and everything on our side goes with it; whatever you kept on your computer is yours.

Honest about the stage.

Needt is pre-launch. There is no SOC 2 report, no penetration test to publish, and saying otherwise would be the first thing to distrust. What exists is what is above: encryption, narrow permissions, and the smallest amount of your day that the job needs. When audits exist, they will be linked here with dates.
