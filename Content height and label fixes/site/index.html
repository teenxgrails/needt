Questions

Questions? Answers.

Does it replace my calendar?

No. It connects to your Google, Apple or Outlook calendar. Your meetings stay where they are, and Needt never moves them.

Where does my data live?

In your Needt account, synced across your devices. Files you choose to keep on your own computer stay there, and we cannot read them. The details are here.

Is it AI?

The planning itself is plain maths: your free hours, how long things take, deadlines, and what depends on what. AI writes the small helpers — a first step for a task, a week summary — and you see them before they are saved.

What if my day doesn't fit?

Needt tells you, and shows what moves. It won't squeeze eight hours of work into four.

Is it for teams?

It's built around one person's day, with a shared view for small teams: who's on what, and what's blocked.

Which platforms?

Needt works in any browser, and you can install it like an app on your computer or phone. Native apps are on the way. How to install it.
