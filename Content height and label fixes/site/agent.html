Agent

You watch it work.

An agent that rearranges your week silently is an agent you stop trusting on the first surprise. So Needt does the opposite of silent: when something moves by itself, the app shows its own hand moving it — the cursor comes out of the chat button, takes the task, drops it into the gap, and goes back.

It asks in words

“Plan my day”, “find me two hours before Thursday”, “what is stuck”. The reply is not a paragraph about what it would do — it is the app doing it, in front of you.

Other assistants write in their own ink

Connect an outside AI assistant and what it writes on your week carries its own colour, so who wrote what needs no byline — you, Needt, or the thing you connected.

Nothing lands without being shown

Every change is drawn before it is saved, and a refusal names the obstacle: “already gone”, or “fixed: 1:1 Anna”. You keep the day, or you do not.

What it is allowed to touch.

Needt may move what it scheduled itself. It may not move an event you agreed to, a block you fixed in place, or a habit that owns its slot — those are the frame it plans around. Every block says which it is: a coloured edge means Needt chose the time and can choose again, a grey edge means it cannot.

What it never sees

Your day, planned. Finally.
