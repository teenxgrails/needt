# Needt — project instructions

## Copy reference

`uploads/CLAUDE-DESIGN-COPY-PROMPT.md` is the **primary reference for all landing copy**
(`site/`). When writing or editing any visible text on the site, follow it: its voice
(plain words, a normal person understands Needt in three seconds), its banned vocabulary,
and its rule of no new numbers.

Standing rules taken from it:

- **No new numbers in copy.** No hours saved, user counts, ratings or percentages beyond
  the ones that document names. A number without a source does not go on the page.
- **Banned from visible copy:** rail, scheduler, placement, placed, MCP, fortnight,
  standing shape.
- **Plain words over product vocabulary.** "It finds the time", not "the scheduler places
  the work".
- Change copy without touching layout, scenes, animations or class names. When a rewritten
  sentence is shorter or longer, move the highlight span onto the word that carries the
  meaning rather than dropping it.

When that document and older copy on the site disagree, the document wins — except on a
matter of product fact (what the app actually does), where the fact wins and the document
should be corrected.
